Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

Obsidian Sync is a personal OAuth integration used with a third-party Obsidian plugin to synchronize notes between personal devices using Google Drive.

2. Google Drive Access

The application requests access to Google Drive files created or explicitly authorized for use by the application.

This access is used to read, create, update, and delete files necessary for synchronizing Obsidian notes, attachments, and configuration data where applicable.

3. Data Storage

Notes and attachments are stored locally on authorized devices and in the user's Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are managed locally by the Obsidian synchronization plugin. No separate application backend is operated to store Google Drive content.

4. Data Sharing

Google Drive data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising purposes.

Data is processed only to provide the synchronization functionality requested by the user.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Synchronized files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the user or the synchronization application.

Users can revoke OAuth access at any time through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already stored in Google Drive.

6. Security

Communication with Google Drive uses HTTPS and Google OAuth 2.0 authentication.

7. Personal Use

This OAuth integration is configured for personal use and is not offered as a commercial or public synchronization service.
